SwiftArchive ZIPX compatibility fixture

This payload verifies streaming reads and extraction through extended ZIP compression methods.
It deliberately contains repeated text so archive tools choose the requested codec instead of storing the file.

Deflate64, BZip2, LZMA, PPMd, XZ, and Zstandard are common extended ZIP methods handled by the bundled backends when the archive advertises them correctly.
